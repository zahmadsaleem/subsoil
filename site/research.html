The cost of fragmented project information

When project records are spread across systems, teams spend time copying files, checking which versions they can use, and verifying what was handed over.

What the studies found

Different tools on each project
47.2% of 999 respondents to an information-sharing question cited differences in methods and tools between projects in a 2025 survey in Japan. {{cite:25}}
Uncertainty about versions
21.9% of the same respondents cited difficulty managing change histories and identifying the latest version. {{cite:25}}
Interoperability costs
$15.8B NIST’s estimate for US capital facilities in 2002. Owners and operators bore about two-thirds. {{cite:21}}
