What the studies found

Tools vary by project
47.2% of 999 respondents to an information-sharing question cited differences in methods and tools between projects in a 2025 survey in Japan. {{cite:2}}
Uncertainty about versions
21.9% of the same respondents cited difficulty managing change histories and identifying the latest version. {{cite:2}}
Reworking shared data
19% of respondents in a 2026 survey of 251 ACEC member firms said they often or very often had to substantially rework data received from clients, partners, or contractors. {{cite:3}}
